- Added responsive constraints, wrapping, 44px interaction targets, reduced-motion handling, lazy/priority image decisions, and desktop/mobile navigation visibility rules. The exact-width browser pass caught and fixed narrow-width overflow in update tags and the hero name.
- Added [`scripts/validate-site-output.rb`](../../scripts/validate-site-output.rb), [`scripts/stage08-browser-check.js`](../../scripts/stage08-browser-check.js), and [`scripts/stage08-cdp-screenshots.js`](../../scripts/stage08-cdp-screenshots.js) for repeatable static and isolated-browser QA.
