## Scientific question

This collaboration asks what can be learned from pediatric clinical EEG and how neural signals can be characterized in the context of clinical and developmental research. The research direction includes EEG questions related to epilepsy, ADHD, autism, and medication effects, alongside broader interests in biomarkers and neural dynamics. These are areas of inquiry, not claims that a biomarker or diagnostic method has been established.

## Approach and role

The work is grounded in clinical EEG and computational analysis. My verified role is Research Collaborator with Dr. Alexander G. Weil and Dr. Aristides Hadjinicolaou at CHU Sainte-Justine. This collaborative line is distinct from my primary doctoral question about dynamic decision-making, while sharing an interest in understanding how neural activity varies over time and across people.

The public material available for this page does not establish a specific cohort, sample size, analysis design, or outcome. I therefore describe the collaboration and its questions without implying results, diagnostic utility, or a particular study protocol.
