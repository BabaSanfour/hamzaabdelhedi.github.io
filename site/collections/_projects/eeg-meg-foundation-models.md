## Scientific question

Can learned representations capture useful structure in EEG and MEG recordings, and under what conditions do those representations generalize across datasets? These questions matter when models are trained on data that differ in recording systems, tasks, populations, or preprocessing. A model that performs well in one setting does not, on its own, establish that it transfers to another.

## Approach and role

This complementary methods line focuses on representation learning, cross-dataset generalization, and evaluation of electrophysiology models. My role includes co-authoring a public roadmap on MEG foundation models. The broader direction is to ask what a model learns and how that claim should be tested, rather than treating a foundation model as a result by itself.

This work supports, but does not replace, the scientific questions in my doctoral decision-making research. No performance claim or project-specific result is presented here.
